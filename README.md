# Hello, I'm Enkhtsetseg (Enji) 👋

Founder of the **Mongolia Circular Battery Initiative (MCBI)**, a Czech-Mongolian initiative working on battery recycling, critical minerals recovery and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) for batteries.

I live in the Czech Republic and work between Europe and Mongolia.

## 🔧 Main projects

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [MCBI-EPR-system](https://github.com/Shirendev1/MCBI-EPR-system) | Pilot portal for battery registration, QR identification and lifecycle tracking · live at [passport.mcbis.org](https://passport.mcbis.org) |
| [mongolia-resource-mirror](https://github.com/Shirendev1/mongolia-resource-mirror) | Open data on Mongolia's natural resource exports: what leaves, where it goes, who gets paid |
| [Circular-Battery-Systems-for-Mongolia](https://github.com/Shirendev1/Circular-Battery-Systems-for-Mongolia) | Research and proposals on circular battery systems for Mongolia |

## 🌍 What I care about
- Circular economy and responsible battery management
- Open data and transparency in natural resources
- Human-centered, ethical AI for small languages like Mongolian
